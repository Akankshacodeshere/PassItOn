How PassItOn works

  1. Log in with your college email. We send you a code.
  2. Post your item with a photo, price and pickup spot.
  3. A student moderator reviews it. Approved posts go live.
  4. Buyers contact you by email. Meet on campus, then mark the item sold.

Will be approved

  • Books, notes, calculators, electronics
  • Lab equipment, hostel items, clothes
  • Cycles, sports gear, kitchen items
  • Clear photo, honest details

Will be rejected

  • Illegal or unsafe items
  • Solved assignments, exam papers
  • Stolen, fake or copied items
  • Phone numbers or links in the post
  • Blurry photos, vague details, scams

Staying safe

  • Meet in busy campus places during the day.
  • Check the item before paying. Never pay in advance.
  • Use Report on anything suspicious. Three reports hide a listing.
  • Each student can have 5 active posts. Posts expire after 30 days and can be reposted.
